Interests

Quantum Gravity

I've been interested in this subject since I began physics. The idea that the two major theories in physics, quantum field theory and general relativity are incompatible is very intriguing to me. I've made it my goal to learn enough about both subjects to understand the difficulty. Thats why I am working so hard to learn quantum field theory and why I spent so much of my undergraduate degree learning general relativity and its prerequisites. I think now, I am nearly ready to begin learning theories of quantum gravity. This is very exciting!

Foundations of Quantum Mechanics

I got interested in foundations after studying Sakurai's and Griffith's textbooks on quantum mechanics. Quantum mechanics is an algorithm for computing observable quantities but it doesn't tell you what is going on physically. This was very interesting to me, because quantum theory is the most fundamental theory in physics and there seems to be a deep mystery on how to interpret it. This led me to read a textbook called The Foundations of Quantum Mechanics by Roderich Tumulka, which taught me about all the different interpretations as well as related ideas like Bell's theorems. This got me very excited to learn more. One of the goals of this website is to allow me to share some of the interesting discoveries I find in the foundations of quantum mechanics.

Traveling for Physics

Over the past six years I've had a lot of fun traveling with my close friend, Gershon Levin, to some very interesting places in the name of physics.

Botswana

This trip started when I couldn't find a professor at McGill University to do research with, in my first year of undergrad. This was becuase I hadn't had the chance to gain enough research experience yet, to be accepted. One day my sister had the great idea, that I should find out about research in Botswana. I went online and found a University called The Botswana International University of Science and Technology (BIUST). On their webpage I found an astrophysics professor named Rhodri Evans and I sent him an Email asking him if we could come to Botswana for astrophysics research. He was enthusiastic and we arranged plans to come. It was a wonderful experience, where we learned about culture, science and collaboration. We also met the professor's two kids named Nunki and Heulwen.

Boston

This trip began after watching all the MIT Lectures from the famous physicist and professor, Walter Lewin. I had began my physics studies on these MIT lectures and I would come back to them from time to time. One day I noticed that Professor Lewin responds to comments under his videos. I decided to comment asking him if we can come to Boston to visit him. He responded a few hours later with a place and time. I was very excited! We planned to take a bus there. But late at night and just 3 days before the meeting, I discovered that all the bus's were canceled except one that was scheduled to leave in just 3 hours. At that point we had no place to stay no american money and as yet no bus ticket. I called up Gershon in a hurry and we made the quickest decision of our lives. We packed and ran making it with only five minutes to spear. That was the beginning of an adveture! The rest of the trip was wonderful. We visted MIT, Harvard and finally met up with professor Walter Lewin in a Dunkin Doughnuts.
