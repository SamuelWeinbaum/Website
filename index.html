Interests

Quantum Gravity

I've been interested in this subject since I began physics. The idea that the two major theories in physics, quantum field theory and general relativity are incompatible is very intriguing to me. I've made it my goal to learn enough about both subjects to understand the difficulty. Thats why I am working so hard to learn quantum field theory and why I spent so much of my undergraduate degree learning general relativity and its prerequisites. I think now, I am nearly ready to begin learning theories of quantum gravity. This is very exciting!

Foundations of Quantum Mechanics

I got interested in foundations after studying Sakurai's and Griffith's textbooks on quantum mechanics. Quantum mechanics is an algorithm for computing observable quantities but it doesn't tell you what is going on physically. This was very intriguing to me, because quantum theory is the most fundamental theory in physics and there seems to be a deep mystery on how to interpret it. This led me to read a textbook called The Foundations of Quantum Mechanics by Roderich Tumulka, which taught me about all the different interpretations as well as related ideas like Bell's theorems. This got me very excited to learn more. One of the goals of this website is to allow me to share some of the interesting discoveries I find in the foundations of quantum mechanics.
